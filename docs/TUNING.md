| Parameter            | What to change                                  | Typical range |
|----------------------|-------------------------------------------------|---------------|
| shear_angle          | Until digits look upright in the mask window    | -6 –12        |
| gap_ratio            | So decimal points fall between boxes            | 0.025 – 0.040 |
| density_thresh       | "Lower if segments are dim, higher if noisy"    | 0.13 – 0.22   |
| NUM_DIGITS           | Real number of digits on your display           | 4 / 5 / 6 …   |
| Right-side crop 0.88 | Increase (e.g. 0.90) if you still catch the dot | 0.85 – 0.92   |
